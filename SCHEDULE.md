# Compost Pilot — Spring Schedule

The schedule below reflects the plan agreed on by the community garden group.

## Weekly Schedule

- **Saturday morning pickups** — collection of incoming compostable material from neighborhood drop-offs.
- **Wednesday evening turns** — turning and aerating the active piles.

## Volunteer Slots

- Each volunteer slot is **90 minutes**.
- Sign up on the board at the garden entrance or reply in the group chat.

## Notes

- Log harvest notes after each Saturday pickup.
- Let the group know if you can't make your slot so a swap can be arranged.
